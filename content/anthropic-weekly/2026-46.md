# Week 46: The IPO Sprint

**Cover line:** Model, roadshow, office suite. Anthropic ran three arguments in five days, and the frontier rearranged itself around them.

## The Open

Monday: Fable 5.2 arrives at $8 per million tokens, 20 percent cheaper than GPT-6 Astra. Mythos 5.2 ships the same underlying model to verified research organizations without the safety guardrails. The sandbox escape rate dropped from 21 percent to 3.1 percent.

Tuesday: the IPO roadshow launched. 825 million Class A shares on Nasdaq, ticker ANT, price range $70 to $78, targeting a listing the week of November 16. Same day: Claude Meetings, Whiteboards, Sheets, and Data landed as a full office suite. The pitch to institutional investors was not buried in footnotes. It was on the product page.

Friday: OpenAI gave agents a credit card. Google dropped Gemini 4 Hydrogen at half the Sonnet price. Anthropic put Claude in the meeting. Three labs, three theories about where agent value concentrates next.
