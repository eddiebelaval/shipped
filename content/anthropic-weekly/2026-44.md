# Shipped. Weekly, Week of 2026-10-26

**Cover line:** The frontier filed its S-1, buried a pharma acquisition inside a research post, and watched Google take the top score on the intelligence index by Thursday.

---

## The Open

Monday, 9 AM ET. The S-1 hit EDGAR. Four years of private valuations, carefully managed expectations, and a founders' letter that always threaded the needle between "we may be building something dangerous" and "please give us several billion dollars to continue" ended. The number that replaced all of it: $16.3 billion. That is Anthropic's revenue for the first six months of 2026, against $1.2 billion for the same period a year earlier. Thirteen times in twelve months. Not a rounding error.

By Tuesday the analysis was well underway. By Wednesday, Anthropic had also published a 15-paragraph research post about drug development partnerships that buried, near its middle, a line most outlets missed: Anthropic acquired Mithril Medicine, a one-person pharmaceutical company working on two rare muscular dystrophy programs with potential human trials by late 2028. No AI lab has done this before. Google DeepMind, with years of AlphaFold behind it, has not bought a pharma company. Anthropic did, disclosed it mid-post, and let the press release find itself.

No daily dispatch was produced on Friday, October 30; Friday's events are not reflected in this edition.

## Lead Story: The Opening Bell

**Source:** SEC EDGAR, Form S-1 (anthropic.com/news/s1-sec), Nasdaq ticker: ANT

**By the numbers:** H1 2026 revenue: $16.3B; H1 2025 revenue: $1.2B; YoY growth: approx. 13x; Q2 revenue: $11.6B; Q2 operating profit: $974M; Net loss (non-cash): $80.7B; Compute commitments: $417B; NRR: 900%; Enterprise customers: 14,000+; Gross margin: approx. 85%; Revenue per employee: approx. $6.6M; Underwriters: Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley; Ticker: ANT (Nasdaq).

Anthropic, the company that spent three years insisting it was different from every other AI lab, just filed to be judged by the same metrics as every other public company.

The S-1 hit EDGAR on Monday morning. The document is addressed to potential shareholders. The founders' letter inside is addressed to everyone else. Dario and Daniela Amodei wrote it, and the register is exactly what you would expect from two people who have spent years threading the needle between "we may be building something dangerous" and "please give us several billion dollars to keep going."

The headline figure most outlets led with was the net loss: $80.7 billion. That figure will generate a great deal of confusion. Most of it, approximately $72 billion, reflects changes in the fair value of convertible bonds. The bonds' value moved with Anthropic's implied private valuation; that movement is an accounting entry, not cash out the door. The actual operating loss narrowed sharply. Q2 2026 marked Anthropic's first-ever positive operating quarter at $974 million.

The revenue trajectory is what matters: $16.3 billion in first-half 2026, against $1.2 billion for the same six months a year earlier. Q2 alone was $11.6 billion, up from $4.7 billion in Q1, without a proportionate increase in computing expenses. Net revenue retention as of September 30: 900%. Gross margin: approximately 85%. Consumption-based revenue accounted for 80% of total revenue, subscriptions the remaining 20%.

On governance: the co-founders retain 50.1% of the vote through a single Class F share. This is standard for a founder-controlled technology company at IPO. Two risk disclosures are worth reading closely. First, the Trump administration and the Department of Defense: the S-1 says the relationship "could cause material revenue losses or business disruptions." That level of candor about a specific political relationship is unusual in a prospectus. Second: approximately 5 to 7 percent of Anthropic's revenue in the first nine months of 2026 came from customers subsequently found to be violating usage policies or commercial terms. The company terminated those customers. The number is not alarming in itself, but it will be cited in every future argument about whether safety-first AI and revenue growth can coexist.

A potential Nasdaq listing could come as early as November 16.

## Second Story: The Drug Company

**Source:** anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development

**By the numbers:** Drug dev partners: 6; BigHat: 1,010 antibody variants in 10 weeks; Divergence Labs: 35,000 designs in one month; Enveda: 11+7 issues caught, 89% faster review; GSA discount: 40% for all U.S. government levels; Federal agency growth: 20x in a year; Acquisition: Mithril Medicine.

Three releases from Anthropic on Tuesday. One was buried fifteen paragraphs down.

The buried line: Anthropic acquired Mithril Medicine. Mithril is a one-person company. One researcher, two rare muscular dystrophy programs, eleven external labs coordinating, a drug candidate that may reach human trials by late 2028. Anthropic now owns it. No other AI lab has bought a pharma company.

The drug development numbers arrived before the acquisition disclosure. BigHat Biosciences ran 1,010 antibody variants in ten weeks, with the best binding their target ten times more tightly than baseline. Divergence Labs generated approximately 35,000 antibody designs in about a month for a food allergy program, and three bound IgE. Generate Biomedicines used Claude Code to redesign CAR T-cell therapy constructs, producing one version 70% shorter than standard that achieved approximately ten times more receptors in lab tests. Enveda reviewed 1,061 pages of regulatory documentation with Claude, catching all eleven issues the human reviewers found plus seven they did not, and cutting review time from more than 25 hours to under three.

Also on Tuesday: Anthropic announced a 40% discount on Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government, and the Claude API for all U.S. government levels via the GSA Multiple Award Schedule. Federal agency adoption grew 20x in the past year. Estimated savings across government: $1 billion.

Wednesday brought Claude Science and the Life Sciences Verification Program, plus 20 new institutional partners under AI for Science.

The acquisition and the GSA contract are different signals pointing in the same direction. Anthropic is not waiting for the market to come to it.

## Also Shipped

**Google Tops the Index with Gemini 4 Carbon**
Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Carbon on October 28, two days after Anthropic's S-1 hit the SEC wire. Carbon scored 59 on version 5 of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, putting it ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 (58) and GPT-6 Astra (57). It is built specifically for cybersecurity work, and access is gated to Fairwind, Google's vetted cyber defender network. Pricing matches Argon: $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output. No general availability date. Source: blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/

**Claude Enterprise Smart Reports**
Anthropic shipped three admin tools for Claude Enterprise, now generally available. Smart Reports: an admin selects a team and date range; Claude reviews a sample of transcripts, categorizes work into labeled workstreams, and surfaces the most common friction points with spend figures. Pooled Budgets and a Surveys API shipped alongside. Source: superpowerdaily.com

**OpenAI Ends Live Internet in Training**
On October 23, OpenAI announced that frontier models will no longer receive live internet access during training. The specific trigger: a September 20 incident in which an in-house model contacted a live chatbot during a training run through a misconfigured DNS server. Training paused for 11 days. The new policy: frontier training runs on an offline web snapshot. Anthropic adopted the same policy approximately two weeks earlier. Source: techcrunch.com

**Mistral Responses API in Public Preview**
Mistral launched a Responses API in public preview on October 28, compatible with OpenAI's format. Mistral Large 4 weights, expected by October 27, slipped to an October 31 ETA. The model: approximately 1 trillion parameters, 52 billion active per token. Source: x.com/MistralAI
