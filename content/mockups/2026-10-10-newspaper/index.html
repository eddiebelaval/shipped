Here is the sequence, because the sequence is the story. Fable 5 and Mythos 5 went generally available on June 9. On the evening of Thursday, June 12, the Commerce Secretary sent Anthropic an export control directive ordering it to suspend all access for any foreign national, inside or outside the United States, including Anthropic's own employees.

"You cannot rebut a classified rationale with a benchmark."

The directive did not say "block this region" or "block these accounts." It followed a nationality, everywhere on earth. There is no clean way to serve a model to some people in a building and not others.

All other Claude models stayed up throughout. Carl Lender, CC BY 2.0.

The blast radius runs straight through the operator layer. Anyone who swapped a production call to Fable 5 spent the weekend staring at a dead endpoint with no published timeline for its return.

The builder's move is unglamorous. Route to Opus 4.8 or Sonnet 4.6 tonight. Treat a model ID as a dependency a government can revoke. Last week the lesson was to watch the meter. This week it is to keep a second model warm.

Image Treatment

Four ways to put a photo in orange ink

Layout and image treatment are separate picks. Any of these can run inside any of the four layouts. All are generated from the source photo by one script, so every issue gets the same hand.

1 · Atkinson

The original Mac dither. Loses some shadow detail on purpose, so highlights blow out to paper. Crisp, graphic, most "printed."

2 · Bayer ordered

A fixed crosshatch grid. Reads as stipple, softer and lighter. Best small, as portraits and thumbnails.

3 · Riso halftone

Round dots on a 45 degree screen, like a newspaper press. Loudest at large sizes, mushy when small.

4 · Ink + orange

Three levels: paper, orange, black ink. Keeps the shadows, so faces and machines stay legible. Most range.
